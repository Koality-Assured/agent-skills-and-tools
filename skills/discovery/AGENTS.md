# Discovery skills

This family contains bounded, evidence-backed discovery workflows for public web and similar external surfaces.

## Rules

- Follow the shared skill contract in [`../skill-conventions.md`](../skill-conventions.md). In the full ai-router checkout, also follow `ai-tooling/AGENTS.md`; standalone repositories follow their destination root `AGENTS.md`.
- Discovery of external systems is read-only and requires explicit human authorization and a single declared host.
- Keep curated discovery cases in the linked `supporting/` source area; do not put secrets or operational data in skills.
